1.  [**Priming - The secret of using ChatGPT**](https://medium.com/@progers1121/how-to-prime-chatgpt-887e7bb28350)

🤖 We all know how powerful ChatGPT is. But do you know how to get the best answers from it? The answer is "priming". Prime is a word commonly used when we are painting walls. First we prime the wall, which means, we get it ready for the final paint color. Priming is all about "getting something ready" to be used.

In terms of ChatGPT, "priming" is the process of getting the model ready for your question. We prepare ChatGPT with enough initial context so that when we ask our questions, it already knows most of the context in which you want an answer.

This article talks about strategies on priming ChatGPT to get the best outcomes.
