1.  [**Tech Jobs Cool Off: Tougher Times Ahead**](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/careersandeducation/bad-news-for-coders-the-us-is-past-peak-software-developer/ar-BB1oGPka)

📉 This article sound very pessimistic, but I would invite you to take a closer look: Actually the tech jobs is still better now than it was in March 2020 right before all hell broke loose with the pandemic.

It just happens that our memory just go back as far as the middle of the pandemic when we saw a surge in the tech space and tech jobs. Now we complain that it is no longer as hot as it was during the pandemic. Of course it is not, at that is because we are going through financial tough times.

How what do we do in times like these: run around like chickens with our heads cut off?? No - We get ready. We get ready for when we find the right opportunity or the right job for us. We don't run scared.

Easier said than done, but it doesn't mean it is not true.
