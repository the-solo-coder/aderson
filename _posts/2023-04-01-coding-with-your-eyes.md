Today I bring games, and good news about the Toronto Tech Industry.

1.  [**Console App Games**](https://github.com/dotnet/dotnet-console-games)

👾 If you have had Programming I or II with me, you know that most of the time we are dealing with the boring console. Not that fun, is it?

What if I tell you that you can play console based games. And you can even see their code. From "**Rock Paper Scissors**" to "**Tic Tac Toe**" and **PacMan**, they have it all. Just about 50 console based games build in C# as console apps with their respective source code.

With these games, you can not only have fun, but you can feel inspired and learn new tricks in C# with the help of console based apps.

Give it a try and let me know.

2.  [**Event: Learning about the Recursion Algorithm with Minesweeper**](https://www.meetup.com/global-visual-studio-code-meetup-group/events/292581142)

💣 There will be a one hour online event this week within the Visual Studio Meetup Group where [Ania Kubow](https://www.youtube.com/@AniaKubow/about) will talk about Recursive Coding using VS Code and applying it to [Minesweeper](https://minesweeper.online/).

Just 1 hour. It would be a very good introduction for anyone who has never been to an online meetup. This is how you start to build your coding career.

3.  [**Toronto Software Engineering Salaries**](https://www.thestar.com/business/2023/03/06/to-software-engineering-salaries-grew-faster-than-those-in-many-us-areas-report.html)

💰 Some good news! This is a short article from Toronto Start that shows the salaries of Software Engineers are growing faster in the Toronto than in other markets in the US.

It doesn't mean that it is higher - It just means that it is growing faster.

We shall celebrate anyways! 🎉
