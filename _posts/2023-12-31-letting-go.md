Today I bring to you coding competitions, how to let go, and making time to learn.

1.  [**Competitive Programming**](https://www.hackerearth.com/getstarted-competitive-programming/)

🏆 Dive into the exhilarating world of competitive programming with this insightful guide.

Just like stepping up to bat in cricket, the author draws a vivid parallel between sports and coding contests, illustrating how perseverance leads to triumph.

From your first simple code to mastering complex algorithms and data structures, this journey is packed with practical advice, engaging tutorials, and challenging contests.

Whether you're a novice coder or aspiring to top the leaderboards, this article is your playbook to sharpening your skills, outsmarting tricky problems, and embracing the thrill of programming competitions. Ready to code your way to victory? Let's begin!

2.  [**Letting Go**](https://nesslabs.com/learning-to-let-go)

🍃 A little something beyond coding for your new year. This enlightening 10-minute read delves into the often challenging yet liberating process of releasing what no longer serves us - be it material possessions, outdated goals, rigid control, lingering relationships, or the heavy shadows of the past.

It guides us through five introspective exercises, each a step towards a lighter, more authentic life.

Discover the art and science behind letting go, and learn to ask the pivotal question, 'Why do I care?', to uncover deeper insights and embrace change.

This article isn't just about decluttering your space; it's about redefining your life's narrative and finding freedom in release.

3.  [**Making time to learn**](https://stackoverflow.blog/2022/12/08/how-to-make-time-for-learning-in-tech)

💡 As we get to the new year, how about we consider making time to learn a new tech?

Unlock the secret to balancing learning and life in the tech world with this article. It delves into the common struggle faced by engineers: the constant juggle between professional development and the demands of work, family, and personal time.

Discover practical tips and strategies to integrate learning into your busy schedule, without sacrificing your personal life.

Learn how to prioritize what to learn, establish effective learning habits, and create a culture of continuous growth both individually and within your organization.

Whether you're a seasoned engineer or just starting out, this article is your guide to staying ahead in the ever-evolving tech landscape while maintaining a balanced life.
