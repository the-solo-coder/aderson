1.  [**AI will not replace developers and here is why**](https://www.kallmanation.com/has-ai-taken-my-job-yet)

🤖 A very common question that I have been getting more and more from students as they question whether there is a future as a developer with all the advancements in AI.

This article delves into the transformative yet unpredictable impacts of AI and the Internet. Nathan Kallman challenges popular expectations, drawing parallels between the Internet's promises and the anticipated influence of AI.

Drawing inspiration from the concept of the Library of Babel, the article explores the challenges of discerning truth in vast information reservoirs, the shortcomings of the digital age, and the evolving job landscape amidst technological advancements.

Dive in to discover if AI truly holds the key to the future we envision.

2.  [**OOP and Python**](https://blog.bytescrum.com/exploring-object-oriented-programming-oop-concepts-in-python-part-1)

🧑‍💻️ I teach Object Oriented Programing (OOP) with the use of C#. If you need a refresher on OOP, here is a fresh one using Python as the language.

Dive deep into the world of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) in Python. This guide meticulously unravels OOP's intricacies, simplifying complex ideas like objects, classes, attributes, and methods. Delve into the constraints of the Procedure-Oriented Programming approach and understand why OOP shines for large-scale, complex projects.

Whether you're a novice coder or a seasoned developer aiming to brush up on OOP nuances, this article is a treasure of knowledge, complete with hands-on examples.

3.  [**Metaverse and the Canadian Economy**](https://www.thecanadianpressnews.ca/business/metaverse-to-contribute-at-least-45-3b-to-canadas-annual-gdp-by-2035-says-meta/article_8b31c891-d841-5a68-be3b-7387567fe2bb.html)

👓The Metaverse's Uncertain Future. Less than two years after Mark Zuckerberg rebranded Facebook to Meta, signaling a future dominated by the Metaverse, adoption and enthusiasm are not that great.

While big companies like Disney and Microsoft are distancing from the virtual realm, recent data from Deloitte suggests the Metaverse could contribute significantly to Canada's GDP by 2035. With tech hubs across Canada delving into virtual innovations, the potential is big.

This article talks about the potential promise of what metaverse can mean for the Canadian economy, and if it comes true, guess who will be building the apps for meta? You!
