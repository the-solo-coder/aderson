2.  [**Pair Programming With ChatGPT**](https://propelauth.hashnode.dev/crosswordgpt-pair-programming-with-gpt-4)

👥 Pair programming is a very powerful tool used by companies and developers. It is a way to have 2 sets of eyes working on the same problem.

It might also be done as a learning tool, when a more and a less experience developer come together to work. This way the less experienced one will benefit from following first-hand how the veteran thinks through a problem.

What if you could pair program with ChatGPT? This is the real power of AI. Not copying code, but learning and building things together.

This article will show how a dev used ChatGPT to build something together. You can apply the same thought process to your projects and have ChatGPT as your learning assistant.

BTW, I'm looking to incorporate more of ChatGPT in my courses next semester. If you have any thoughts of how you think we could use AI in class, just hit reply. I'm sure you have used on some projects already, haven't you? 🤫
