After reading this, you will start to look at fonts in a different way.

3.  [**Context Switching**](https://www.loom.com/blog/cost-of-context-switching)

Context switching is when you keep changing your focus constantly. You think that you are "multitasking", but you are actually loosing your ability to go deeper on the task at hand.

**Changing focus frequently** while you are studying or working on a project **has a cost**.

This article sheds some light into why doing multiple things are the same time or changing focus too often is not a good practice.
