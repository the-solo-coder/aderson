1.  [**Event: What is Bad Code?**](https://www.meetup.com/reactto/events/291048792/)

📅 People ask me all the time if I have tips for them on how to get a job as a coder. One of the most useful tips I can give is to join a meetup.

Today I come to you with an in-person event on Feb 23, in case you are in the Toronto area (if you are not, you can find an event in your area if you look at [meetup.com](http://meetup.com))

This is a meet up focused on React, but even if you don't know anything about React, I recommend you going to the event.

If you check their agenda, they have time for networking and the presentation itself is just about 30 minutes. Yes, you will get some useful tech information, but I recommend you go and focus on networking.

*"But I'm shy, I don't know how to network"*. Get a grip. Just approach someone and ask what they do. That is what is expected in those events. The conversation will flow from there. If not, move on and approach someone else. I know I'm simplifying it too much, but it is that simple.

I will not be joining this event because I have class to teach at the same time, but I would otherwise. Final note: **I got my last job because of a meetup**.

2.  [**What is a headless CMS?**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=av2CL4nusg4)

💆 CMS is the acronym for Content Management System. I'm talking about tools like WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Joomla, Drupal, and my all-time favorite DNN ([I worked with it quite a lot](https://www.youtube.com/@dnnhero)) - If you are still not sure what a CMS is, check [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=deqX0gMeUVc).

A lot of coders, like us, end up working with these kinds of tools through our careers.

This is a simple and quick video (4 min) that will expand your understanding of CMSs and will show you what a headless CMS is, which has become very popular in the last few years.

Basically in a **headless CMS, the backend code is very loosely coupled with the frontend**, connected only by simple APIs. [  
    ](https://martinfowler.com/tags/domain%20driven%20design.html%5D.￼)
