2.  [Boost Your Coding with a Dev Journal!](https://stackoverflow.blog/2024/05/22/you-should-keep-a-developer-s-journal)

📖 A developer's journal offers clarity in the complex world of coding. It's a space to clearly define problems and track successful strategies.

Embrace the practice to streamline your thoughts and enhance your coding efficiency. Capture every detail from ideas to emotions, ensuring nothing hampers your focus.

This could also be the beginning of your blogging. Why not? Get two for the price of one.

3.  [**Learn the basics of APIs**](https://levelup.gitconnected.com/api-design-101-from-basics-to-best-practices-a0261cdf8886)

🛠️ In this article, the author guides readers through the essentials of API creation, using an e-commerce example to illustrate key concepts.

The article covers CRUD operations, communication protocols, and different API paradigms like REST, GraphQL, and gRPC. It also discusses best practices for ensuring backward compatibility and setting rate limits.

This article is essential for developers who want to enhance their skills in API design. It provides practical insights and best practices for creating efficient, scalable, and user-friendly APIs.

By understanding the basics and advanced techniques, developers can improve their code quality, ensure smoother data communication, and create APIs that are robust and easy to maintain.
