2.  [**Programming is everything and everything is programming**](https://blog.nikfp.com/programming-is-everything-and-everything-is-programming)

♾️ This is quite an inspiring article for beginners and written by a beginner dev, which is even more impressive as he provides great insights. It is a light reading. It traces a **parallel between life and coding** and talks about **how get started** during a time of financial turmoil and uncertainties like the one we are going through now. [  
    ](https://martinfowler.com/tags/domain%20driven%20design.html%5D.￼)

3.  [**Focusing Fun**](https://abbeyperini.hashnode.dev/transforming-tired-tabbing)

🎯 This article focusses on "focus"! Not "focus" as in "what I'm working right now", but on **web interface focus**. For example, when you are typing something in a web form, you have the cursor focused on the input box.

The article talks about how to best demonstrate focus using CSS styling.
