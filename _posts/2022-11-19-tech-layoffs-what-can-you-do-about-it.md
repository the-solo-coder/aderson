3.  [**Learn JSON**](https://youtu.be/iiADhChRriM)

💾 This video will cover what you need to know about **JSON in 10 minutes**. It will cover what JSON is, why JSON is important, what JSON is used for, the syntax of JSON, and multiple examples of JSON.

This might be a basic topic for some, but I think it is worth mentioning.

JSON is the most popular data representation format, and is one of the most important, and easiest concepts you can learn in programming. It allows you to create APIs, config files, and structured data.
