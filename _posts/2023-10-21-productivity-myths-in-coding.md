1.  [**Little Lies in Coding**](https://fibbonachos.hashnode.dev/unraveling-productivity-myths-in-software-development)

🤥 Are you ready to challenge the status quo and unlock the true secrets to productivity in software development? In a world where time is money and innovation is paramount, this article exposes the myths about coding productivity that might be holding you back.

Discover why longer hours don't always equate to higher productivity, why multitasking can be a silent productivity killer, and why every developer brings a unique value that cannot be interchanged. It will also show why measuring productivity by lines of code is a fallacy, and why high productivity alone doesn't guarantee high quality.

Buckle up for a journey into the heart of software development productivity and embark on a path toward sustainable success. Read on to separate fact from fiction and revolutionize your approach to achieving software development excellence!

2.  [**What else can we do on React?**](https://dpkreativ.hashnode.dev/cool-things-to-do-with-react-outside-web-development)

💪 Discover the hidden powers of React beyond web development! Dive into this exciting article that uncovers six incredible non-web use cases for React.

From creating dynamic videos and crafting responsive email templates to generating PDFs and building command-line interfaces, React proves its versatility in ways you never imagined.

Explore these unique applications and unlock a world of innovative possibilities with React. Watch the embedded video for a deeper dive into each fascinating use case!
