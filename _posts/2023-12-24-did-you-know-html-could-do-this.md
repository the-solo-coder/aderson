Today I bring to you HTML, Microservices, and the types of people using AI to code.

1.  [**HTML can do this?**](https://dev.to/yuridevat/html-can-do-this-part-1-3ab2)

🔍 Discover the untapped potential of HTML. This enlightening article peels back the layers of HTML, revealing features often missed by many.

It showcases how to create interactive widgets effortlessly without JavaScript, and delves into the art of embedding direct email and phone links in web pages.

You will learn about the transformative power of the \<details\> tag and the versatility of the \<a\> tag for streamlined communication. The piece also introduces the \<datalist\> for an autocomplete functionality and highlights simple methods for text modifications.

Ideal for both experienced developers and newcomers, this series is set to deepen your HTML knowledge and contribute to a more accessible web. Get ready to unlock these hidden HTML capabilities!

2.  [**Microservices Interview Questions**](https://blog.bytebytego.com/p/7-microservices-interview-questions)

❓ Are you curious (or confused) about the world of microservices and how it is changing the software industry. This article unravels the complexities and innovations of microservices.

This cutting-edge approach to software development is reshaping how companies handle growing complexity and cloud computing. It explores key concepts, tackle common interview questions, and hear insights from experts like Martin Fowler and Adrian Cockcroft.

Whether you're preparing for an interview or just eager to learn more about this pivotal technology trend, this article is your gateway to understanding the microservices revolution.

It dissects everything from the basic definition of microservices to the intricate challenges they solve and introduce in the tech world!

3.  [**AI and the 3 Types of Programmers**](https://stackoverflow.blog/2023/12/11/three-types-of-ai-assisted-programmers)

🤖 Dive into the fascinating world of AI-assisted programming! This insightful article explores the impact of large language models like ChatGPT on the programming landscape.

From enthusiastic LinkedIn influencers to seasoned developers, everyone's talking about how AI is reshaping coding. But it's not all smooth sailing; the anticipated revolution of AI-built apps hasn't quite taken off as expected.

The article delves into the nuances of using AI in programming, highlighting the varied implications for different skill levels—from the no-code entrepreneur to the seasoned engineer.

It's not just about using AI, but how you use it that counts.

Whether you're a coding novice or a veteran, this piece sheds light on the potential and pitfalls of AI in programming, urging a thoughtful approach to this powerful tool.
