1.  [**A "Coderpreneur" Story**](https://marclou.beehiiv.com/p/my-solopreneur-story-0-to-65k-month-in-2-years)

🚀 I love stories of developers that succeeded as a solo entrepreneur. This is a nice story.

Marc Lou's journey from a fired product manager to a successful solopreneur is nothing short of inspiring.

After losing his job in 2021, Marc dove headfirst into the world of startups, driven by a dream of financial independence and creative freedom. His story is a remarkable testament to the power of resilience and hard work.

Over the course of two years, he launched an astonishing 17 products, grew a newsletter to 7,000 readers, and amassed a Twitter following of 55,000.
