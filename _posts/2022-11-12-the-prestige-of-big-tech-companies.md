2.  [**What's the big deal with FAANG?**](https://chiommy.hashnode.dev/faang-whats-the-big-tech-idea)

🏆 At some point in the past I had the desire to work for Microsoft. I was still living in Brazil (over 20 years ago) and I was able to get an interview over the phone with a Microsoft recruiter from Redmond, Washington.

I was not selected. I was disappointed of course but I knew I could barely speak English at that point in time, so it was no surprise I wasn't the right choice.

Why did I share this? Because I know the lure that big tech companies have on a lot of tech people, including me back then. This blog post will talk about the **FAANG companies and why they are so desirable**.

Different people, sites and publications will also mention about other acronyms renaming Facebook to Meta, removing Netflix, adding Microsoft, etc. It doesn't matter that much. In the end, we are talking about the tech giants.
