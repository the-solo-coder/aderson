2.  [**Online Browser Based Coding Editor**](https://nasserspace.hashnode.dev/vscode-file-system-access-api)

💾 Do you know that you can use Visual Studio Code (VS Code) online in the browser? This article talks about how your browser can manipulate local files by using the File System Access API.

Beyond that, now you know you can use [VS Code in the browser](https://vscode.dev/) without installing anything locally.

3.  [**What is a "Product Manager"?**](https://www.atlassian.com/agile/product-management/product-manager)

👷  A product manager is the person who identifies the customer need and the larger business objectives that a product or feature will fulfill, articulates what success looks like for a product, and unites a team to turn that vision into a reality.

And guess what? I decided to become one.

This article will tell you what a Product Manager does. It can be a career path for you.
