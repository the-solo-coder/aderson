Today I bring to you how to sit right, IDEs, and frontend basics.

1.  [**Desk Posture Made Easy**](https://stephenbugden.com/a-guide-to-maintaining-a-good-sitting-posture-at-your-desk/)

🪑Are you slouched over your desk, lost in the digital world, forgetting the importance of posture? Dive into this guide put together by my friend Stephen Bugden, to transform your workspace into a haven of ergonomic bliss.

Discover the secrets of perfect posture, from the ideal alignment of your spine to the best position for your arms and legs, and embark on a journey towards comfort and efficiency. With practical tips and personal insights, learn how to adjust your chair, desk, and accessories to keep discomfort at bay and boost your productivity.

From the personal trials of a software developer to the science of ergonomics, explore how simple changes can alleviate pain and enhance your work experience. Say goodbye to hunching and hello to a healthier, happier you!

2.  [**Code Wizards: IDEs Unleashed!**](https://stackoverflow.co/teams/resources/ai-industry-guide/our-ai-journey/ide/)

🧙‍♂️ Dive into the heart of a developer's universe, where the choice of where to craft code is as pivotal as the code itself.

From the simplicity of a text editor to the complex embrace of an IDE, each tool plays a crucial role in sustaining a developer's flow state, ensuring their creative journey is uninterrupted.

Explore how these environments shape the art of coding, from the bare-bones elegance of Emacs to the multifaceted prowess of modern IDEs.

Discover how IDEs not only facilitate code writing but also excel in managing the intricate dance of building and deploying applications, making a developer's life significantly smoother.

3.  [**The Basics of Frontend Dev**](https://blog.stackademic.com/we-forgot-frontend-basics-2f9a1c4dabaa)

🚦 This post discusses how frontend development has become overcomplicated with endless trends and paradigms, and highlights the importance of mastering basic skills, such as vanilla JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.

It also provides tips for improving frontend development skills.
