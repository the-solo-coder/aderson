I'm a very positive person but sometimes we have to discuss things that are not that good. In today's newsletter you will find an article about layoffs in tech.

Here are the 3 things I have selected for you this week:

1. [**No Code / Low Code – What is that?**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jaRCENYBuYo)

   I have selected a video from the [TechLead](https://www.youtube.com/c/TechLeadShow) guy (I think he’s funny and obnoxious 🤪) to give you a sense of what this is and what it means for us developers.

   Don’t worry, **we will not loose our jobs** because of “no code” – Coders will still be required for a very long time.

   This topic is not new. Discussions about it were already happening 20 years ago and look where we are: We didn’t loose our jobs yet.

2. [**Layoffs in Tech**](https://thecanadian.news/thousands-were-laid-off-from-tech-companies-this-summer-we-spoke-to-several-workers-to-find-out-what-it-was-like)

   It's not all roses and rainbows 🌈 in the technology industry. In a post-pandemic world with rising inflation, tech companies are tying their seatbelts and that usually means layoffs.

   This is believed to be a **rebalancing period** and in the long run the industry will keep growing strong.

   This article was originally posted at the [Toronto Star](https://www.thestar.com/business/technology/2022/08/03/thousands-were-laid-off-from-tech-companies-this-summer-we-talked-to-several-workers-to-find-out-what-it-was-like.html) but I don't think anyone here has a subscription to it, so I linked to the ["open"](https://thecanadian.news/thousands-were-laid-off-from-tech-companies-this-summer-we-spoke-to-several-workers-to-find-out-what-it-was-like) version.

3. [**Java: 25 years young and ready for another 25**](https://github.com/readme/featured/java-programming-language)

   So many of you studied a lot of Java during college and sometimes you might be thinking: *“Why are we studying a language that is dying??”*.

   I’m no Java programmer and I’m not a fan in particular (I got a Java Certification right before coming to Canada 20 years ago, but don’t tell that to anyone 😊) but the **market for Java Developers seems to be stronger than ever**.

   Just because a technology is no longer the flavor of the month, it doesn't mean it is irrelevant.
