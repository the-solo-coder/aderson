2.  [**CSS Minification**](https://blog.sentry.io/why-dont-we-talk-about-minifying-css/)

🗜 Minifying CSS boosts website performance by making files smaller and faster to load. Modern tools and frameworks handle this automatically, but understanding the process can still benefit developers.

Today's tech stacks, like HTTP/2 and front-end frameworks, optimize CSS delivery, reducing the need for manual minification. Developers can focus on other tasks while ensuring quick, efficient website loading times.

3.  [**Developers: Fighting Fires More Than Innovating!**](https://medium.com/@maha-/developers-spend-close-to-57-of-their-time-firefighting-than-innovating-cisco-survey-98243c963c9c)

🔥 A lot of new developers only realize what we really do in our jobs, once they get to their first job. They should know that we spend over half of our time fixing application issues rather than creating new features.

A Cisco study reveals the need for better tools to help developers focus on innovation and improve performance.

The study highlights that developers face increased pressure to deliver fast, seamless digital experiences. Full-stack observability and AI are seen as key solutions to reduce troubleshooting time and boost productivity.
