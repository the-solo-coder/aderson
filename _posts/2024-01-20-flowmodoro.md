3.  [**Time Management for Developers**](https://dev.to/flowmodor/why-pomodoro-doesnt-work-try-this-alternative-2no9)

🍅 I have been a fan of the [Pomodoro Technique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique) for many many years, but I admit: I have abandoned it in 2018 when I started to teach, as in class you cannot take 5 minutes break every 30 min (actually you could, but I never tried it).

Discover why the traditional Pomodoro Technique may not be the best fit for everyone, especially developers, and explore a more flexible alternative—Flowmodoro.

While Pomodoro's rigid intervals can interrupt the coveted "flow state" essential for coding, Flowmodoro counts up instead of down, allowing you to work until you naturally require a break.

Learn how to implement Flowmodoro and enhance your coding experience.
