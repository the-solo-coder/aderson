2.  [**Backup Anxiety**](https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter/guides/how-to-back-up-your-computer/)

💽 I admit, I have a bit of "backup anxiety". I'm always wondering when will be the day that I will try to find a project file, documents that I scan or photos, and I cannot find them.

I have partially solved it with things like Evernote (old school) and DropBox. But I still have legacy backups with external hard-drives, CD/DVDs, and previous services like Backblaze and Amazon Photos (or something like that).

This article goes through a few options and strategies. From Mac to Windows, you will find free and paid options here.

🤖 We talk so much about using ChatGPT that we forget to understand how we can use it connected directly to our code.

In this recorded-from-live session, you will get to know how you can connect to ChatGPT and use it within your code using C# and .NET (remember, I'm a Microsoft fanboy).
