*<sub>I want to share a quick communication experience I had recently. I was part of a 1-hour presentation at work, and my section was about 15 minutes. To stay on track and not go over time, I wrote out exactly what I wanted to say for each slide—with a little help from ChatGPT.</sub>*

*<sub>It went well overall, but my manager said I was “a little too animated” 🙂. That’s just me—I talk with my hands and can’t help adding some extra energy, especially when I’m reading something out loud. I guess since I wasn’t improvising, all my expression went into the gestures.</sub>*

*<sub>But here’s the cool part: what I just wrote? That was my 5-minute communication journal for the day. And that’s exactly what we’re talking about today—how a simple daily journal can help you track and improve your communication, one entry at a time.</sub>*

**What if I told you that you could become a better communicator in just 5 minutes a day?** Most people want to improve how they speak, write, or explain things—but they don’t know where to start. And if you’re like most tech folks, you’re busy. You don’t have time to take a 12-week speaking course or rehearse in front of a mirror for an hour. But what if the secret was as simple as picking up a pen?

Let’s break down how a 5-minute daily communication journal can train your brain to express yourself more clearly, confidently, and effectively—without needing a mic or an audience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Why This Works

🧠 **You build awareness** Most of us communicate on autopilot. Journaling helps you step back and reflect:

- What worked in that conversation?

- What didn't land well?

- What would you try differently next time?

By writing this down, you start to see patterns—and patterns are where the gold is.

🛠️ **You practice without pressure** No manager. No team. No Zoom. Just you, your thoughts, and a notebook. This is your space to try new phrases, rewrite that awkward Slack message, or replay how you could’ve asked that question more clearly. Low-stakes practice is how real skill grows.

📈 **You track your growth** Imagine flipping back through your entries and realizing: “Wait—I *am* getting better!” That moment is powerful. Journaling lets you witness your own evolution, one line at a time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### How to Start Your 5-Minute Journal

📝 **Pick one of these daily prompts:**

- What’s one thing I said today that didn’t land the way I intended? How would I say it differently next time?

- Did I avoid saying something because I didn’t know how to express it clearly? Why?

- Who did I explain something to today? What analogy or example did I use?

- How was my tone in today’s team meeting—did it match the moment?

- Did I say too much? Too little?

You don’t have to answer all of them. Just pick one. Five minutes. That’s it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### When and Where to Journal

- **Morning Reflection**: Write about a conversation or interaction you’re planning today. Think through how you’ll approach it.

- **Post-Meeting Debrief**: After a team meeting or 1:1, jot down how it went. Did you get your point across?

- **End-of-Day Wind Down**: Before bed or as part of your shutdown ritual, reflect on one communication moment that stood out.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Make It a Habit

- **Keep it visible**: Put your notebook somewhere you can’t ignore it.

- **Pair it with something**: After your morning coffee. Right before checking email. Stack it on top of an existing habit.

- **Don’t aim for perfect**: This is *your* space. Scribble. Bullet. Brain dump. Doesn’t matter. What matters is that you *show up*.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### What You’ll Notice Over Time

- You pause and *think* before speaking

- You catch yourself rambling—and stop

- You explain things in simpler, more relatable ways

- You become more intentional with your tone

- You feel *less anxious* about difficult conversations

Because you’ve rehearsed. In writing. Over and over again.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Final Thoughts

You don’t need more time. You need more intention. A simple 5-minute communication journal isn’t magic—but it *is* a powerful tool. The kind that builds awareness, strengthens clarity, and helps you find your voice—one entry at a time.
