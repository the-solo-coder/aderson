Today I bring to you AI coding, dev mindset , and a tech event in Toronto.

1.  [**Better coding with AI**](https://www.figma.com/blog/what-codegen-is-actually-good-for/)

🤖 This insightful article sheds light on the actual utility and potential of codegen tools. Contrary to popular belief, codegen is not about entirely automating the design-to-code transition but enhancing it.

The article explores the vast ecosystem of codegen tools, from simple code completion in IDEs to modern AI-driven systems. Notably, as AI continues its rapid advancement, many are eager to harness its potential. Yet, a mere 3% of developers fully trust the accuracy of AI-driven codegen.

The piece delves deep into balancing automation with customization, the value of understanding designs, and how to maximize the benefits of codegen.

2.  [**The Adventures of Learning in Engineering**](https://juraj.hashnode.dev/good-engineers-train-their-skills-great-engineers-train-their-mindset)

🛠️ Embarking on a career path isn't just about mastering skills, but evolving our thinking. From writing code to managing teams, every phase presents its unique challenges. When we understand these phases, we can approach them with a more positive and growth-oriented attitude.

Dive into the 3 major phases that define our professional journey and discover how to harness each stage's potential.

Ever felt overwhelmed or doubted yourself in a new job? You're not alone. Whether it's struggling with complex tasks or communicating with teammates, every professional faces hurdles. The key isn't just overcoming them but growing from them.

Unravel the steps to turn every challenge into an opportunity, and why the right mindset makes all the difference.

Attending tech meetups bolsters a developer's career by facilitating networking, enhancing skills, keeping them updated with industry trends, boosting visibility, and offering direct job opportunities, all while fostering a sense of community and shared growth.
