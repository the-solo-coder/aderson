1.  [**Learning How To Learn**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O96fE1E-rf8)

This is not about tech, but about learning, which we are constantly doing in the tech space if we want to progress. 👨‍🎓👩‍🎓

I came across Barbara Oakley a few years ago recommended by a good friend. I took her course called "[Learning How to Learn](https://www.coursera.org/learn/learning-how-to-learn)" and became a fan.

Tech is a very fast paced industry (the fastest) and if you don't know good learning strategies, it will be very hard for you to keep up. Today I'm sharing a [TEDx talk by Barbara](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O96fE1E-rf8) and [a link to her book](https://www.amazon.ca/Learn-Like-Pro-Science-Based-Anything/dp/1250799376).

**If you want to develop better learning strategies**, you should check it out. It is not as complicated as you may think.

2.  [**Microsoft Ignite**](https://ignite.microsoft.com)

This is an **annual conference** for developers and IT professionals hosted by Microsoft.

The conference will happen in-person and online from October 12-14. It is **free** to attend it online. 🎫 The event consists of presentations, sessions and hands-on labs. It offers opportunities to meet Microsoft experts, MVPs and community members.

There is something there for every area of interest that you may have.[
