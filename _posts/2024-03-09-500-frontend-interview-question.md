2.  [**What is Web 3.0?**](https://jayasuriyan.hashnode.dev/what-you-need-to-know-about-web-30-as-a-frontend-developer)

➡️ As the internet evolves, the concept of Web 3.0 is becoming a hot topic, especially after Mark Zuckerberg's announcement about Meta's metaverse project.

This shift towards a new digital universe is not just about fancy tech buzzwords; it's about the transformation of how we interact with the internet.

From static pages of the early '90s to the interactive platforms of today, we're stepping into an era where users will own and control their digital content through blockchain technology.
