Today I bring to you debugging, a thousand languages, and a hackathon.

1.  [**Debugging & Troubleshooting**](https://blog.hartleybrody.com/debugging-code-beginner/)

🚨 This article offers practical advice for troubleshooting and fixing code.

It covers techniques such as printing variable values, starting with existing working code, running code frequently, reading and Googling error messages, guessing and checking, commenting out code, and taking breaks.

The tips aim to help beginners efficiently identify and resolve issues in their code, making the debugging process less frustrating.

2.  [**Top 1,000 Computer Languages**](https://pldb.io/lists/top1000.html)

🔝 This page lists the most popular programming languages based on various metrics such as foundation score, number of users, and number of jobs.

Each language entry includes information about its creator, year of appearance, tags, and other relevant data points like inbound links and measurements.

This helps developers making informed decisions about learning and using specific programming languages.
