2.  [**Pinky Promise**](https://blog.delpuppo.net/a-promise-is-forever)

🤞 Dive into the dynamic realm of web development with our enlightening guide on JavaScript Promises!

Grasp the essence of asynchronous operations and how they're revolutionizing the way we manage tasks without hindering the main thread's flow.

Say goodbye to the chaotic "callback hell" and welcome a sleek, efficient approach to coding with the ingenious use of Promises.

This essential tool is reshaping error handling and code readability, making it an indispensable asset for contemporary JavaScript developers.

3.  [**Anguilla's AI Goldmine: Small Island, Big Bucks**](https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-domains)

🌴 Dive into the story of Anguilla, a small Caribbean gem, transforming its luck with the AI craze. The island, known for its pristine beaches and a population of just 15,000, is now on the global map for a different reason. Its .ai domain has become a hot commodity among AI companies, bringing unexpected prosperity.

Meet Vince Cate, the man behind this digital gold rush. From starting an email service in 1994 to managing .ai domain sales, Vince has seen Anguilla's fortunes change firsthand. The AI boom has catapulted .ai registrations, making it a significant contributor to the island's economy.

Discover how this tiny nation is navigating its windfall with smart strategies, setting an example for others to follow.
