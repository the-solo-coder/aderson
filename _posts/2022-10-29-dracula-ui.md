Today I'm bringing to you an article about a person who loves to help, a spooky tool and a question: "Are we Engineers?".

1.  [**Motivated to Serve**](https://stackoverflow.blog/2022/10/09/how-to-earn-a-million-reputation-on-stack-overflow-be-of-service-to-others)

🆘 This article tells the story about "VonC". Most likely a mix of his real name plus last name. A few weeks ago, him with 4 other members got over the 1 million reputation mark on [stackoverflow](https://stackoverflow.com). This is a HUGE milestone that took 14 years for him to get to. Not that he was motivated by this number, but the community celebrated anyway.

He said that he wanted to learn more about technologies and be of service to people who might not have the same access as he does.

You don't need to be an expert to help others. **You become an expert by helping others.** I hope this can potentially inspire you a bit as well to maybe think about ways to help. It can change your career.
