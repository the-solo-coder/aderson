2.  [**Code Conventions**](https://jorzel.hashnode.dev/the-power-of-conventions-in-software-development)

📐 Conventions can serve as a type of contract between team members that allows solving problems in a predictable and designed way.

I'm fan of stablishing and adhering to coding conventions. I preach it to students and I follow them in my job.

It just makes things easier for everyone involved. Remember that the code you write today can be maintained by someone else tomorrow.

This article talks about the importance of standards in Software Development. Any dev should keep this in mind.

3.  [**Software Engineers & ADHD**](https://stackoverflow.blog/2023/06/05/what-developers-with-adhd-want-you-to-know/)

🔎 ADHD stand for Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder. My daughter has ADHD, so I have first-hand experience with someone that has a hard time to focus (isn't it all of us?).

This article from Stack Overflow is based on a conversation with two software engineers that were diagnosed with ADHD. It tries to break the stigma around the topic. The developers have chosen to remain anonymous.
